Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no credits or watermarks and you don't need an account. The AI runs in your browser so each image costs nothing to process.

Why is the first image slower?

The first time you use it your browser downloads the AI model. That is about 220 MB for Best quality or 46 MB for Fast. It is saved on your device so it starts much faster next time.

What resolution do I get?

Up to Full HD (1920 × 1080). Smaller images keep their original size.

The edges aren't perfect. What can I do?

Use the Erase and Restore brushes in the editor to paint away leftovers or bring back parts of your subject.
