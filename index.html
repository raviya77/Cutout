Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. There are no credits, no watermarks and no account. The AI runs in your browser, so each image costs nothing to process.

Why is the first image slower?

The first time, your browser downloads the AI model (about 220 MB for Best quality, or 46 MB for Fast). It's saved on your device, so after that it starts instantly — even offline.

What resolution do I get?

Up to Full HD (1920 × 1080). Smaller images keep their original size.

The edges aren't perfect. What can I do?

Use the Erase and Restore brushes in the editor to paint away leftovers or bring back parts of your subject.
